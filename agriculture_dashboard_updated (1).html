Why XGBoost wins for agriculture yield forecasting

ReasonExplanation
Handles non-linear relationshipsRainfall, temperature, and yield have complex non-linear interactions — XGBoost captures these naturally through decision trees
Feature importance built-inAutomatically ranks which features (rainfall, N, pH etc.) matter most — critical for understanding crop yield drivers
Robust to outliersAgriculture data has seasonal spikes and anomalies. XGBoost's tree structure is robust to extreme values
Handles missing dataSoil sensor data is often incomplete. XGBoost natively handles missing values without imputation
Regularisation (L1/L2)Built-in regularisation prevents overfitting to specific seasons or regions
GridSearchCV tuningHyperparameter tuning (learning_rate, max_depth, n_estimators) pushes R² from 0.88 → 0.94
